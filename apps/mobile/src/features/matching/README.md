# matching

The queue, filters, likes, budgets, recycling. TD-11, TD-12, TD-14, ADR-062.

The queue (#379, #502, ADR-034, ADR-062): `QueueScreen`, `useQueue`, the queue's client; the route stays `/queue` and the API's stay under `/queue`, the day's log of it. `useQueue` keeps the cards it holds, and once none is left to decide it reads on: what comes goes after them by place, so that Undo's last card stays, and only an empty answer is the queue's end. While that read runs the screen says it is loading, and a read that fails is the failed state with its retry, which keeps what the phone holds. The end asks once more each time it comes back in sight, since a change of filters or home works on the next read (ADR-062 §8): the button into What you seek leads back to it. A read that finds another day replaces the cards with no word, since nothing was lost (#581); only a press that was lost is told. Later is the decision `skip`, a text button in the decision bar in every placement, and its answer's `later` says whether it put the person on the Later list (`queue.later.first` the first time, `queue.later.full` at the list's most); the buttons float over the card, each on its own fill, and the card scrolls under them to a spacer of their height (#681); a like or a fish refused with `your_turn_limit` keeps its card, and the notice says how many wait now, from the conversation list without thumbs, since a read of the queue would serve its cards again, or the `yourTurn` the queue holds when that read fails. `QueueNotice` is a catalogue key, or that number.

The Later list (#575, ADR-064, #681): each person on it is a star, put there by Later in the API, so the phone has no star button. `StarredScreen` is the list at `/stars`, reached by `StarsButton` on home and at the queue's end while it shows somebody (`useStars`), the queue's end replacing the queue with it; a row opens `/stars/{id}`, which is `QueueScreen` given `star`: the same hook and card for that one card, and the way back to the list after it. The list signs nothing; a row's thumb is asked for as any photo's.

The short pool (#99, ADR-042, ADR-062 §10): at the queue's end `ShortPool` reads what may be relaxed for today and offers a group for each way under a heading that says it, each offer a button with the field and how much it widens the pool, its whole sentence its spoken label (#581); a tap appends the first cards it added (`useQueue`'s `relax`), the read after them goes on to the rest, and a relaxed card names its deal-breaker and undoes it in one tap (`unrelax`). Every card names the person's deal-breakers its person did not answer, and carries the band its entry was written with (ADR-061 §5), which `QueueScreen` hands `ProfileCardView`: nothing on the phone works one out. The likes left (#524, ADR-036 §6): each read and each decision's answer carry `likesLeft`, and the decision bar says it in words, in every placement of the buttons; nothing announces it after a decision.
